Our Story

Brewed with intention
since 2017.

What started as a tiny espresso bar on a quiet side street has become a place thousands of people call their second home.

Mission & Vision

Our mission is simple: serve the best cup of coffee you'll have all week, in a space that feels unmistakably yours. We believe in fair-trade sourcing, low-waste operations, and that good coffee deserves good company.

Our vision is to build a network of neighborhood cafés that elevate the local craft scene — one bean, one barista, one regular at a time.

Our Journey

From a single shop to a community

Seven years, a lot of late nights, and a small mountain of beans.

2017
The first espresso bar

Opened a 12-seat counter with a single La Marzocco and a stubborn dream.

2019
Roastery launched

Started roasting in-house — twice weekly, never more than 48 hours old.

2021
Second location

Opened our flagship café with co-working tables and a community library.

2023
Direct-trade partnerships

Signed long-term agreements with five farms across three continents.

2025
250,000 cups poured

And counting. Thank you for every single one.
