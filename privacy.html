Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email, mobile number).
  • Order history and delivery addresses you save.
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, device type) to improve the site.

How we use your information

  • To process orders and provide customer support.
  • To send order confirmations, receipts, and important updates.
  • To personalize recommendations and improve our menu.
  • To send marketing emails — only if you opt in.

Sharing your data

We never sell your data. We only share it with trusted service providers (payment processors, delivery partners) to fulfill your order.

Your rights

You can request a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. Contact privacy@codewithcoffee.cafe.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart. You can clear cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Contact

Questions? Reach out to us at our contact page.
